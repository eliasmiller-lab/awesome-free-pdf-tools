## ✨ Key Features

- **100% Client-Side Processing**: Your confidential documents never leave your device or get uploaded to third-party cloud servers.
- **Lightning Fast**: Powered by WebAssembly (Wasm) and modern JavaScript engines for instant execution.
- **No Sign-Up Required**: Zero registration, no hidden watermarks, no daily limits or credit card traps.
- **Cross-Platform**: Runs in modern web browsers across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android.

## 🔒 Why In-Browser PDF Tools?

Traditional online PDF converters upload your files to remote cloud servers for conversion. This poses substantial risks:
1. **Privacy & Compliance**: Confidential contracts, financial statements, and personal identity documents could be stored or logged on unknown servers.
2. **Speed & Bandwidth**: Uploading and downloading large PDF files consumes bandwidth and takes noticeable time.
3. **Availability**: In-browser tools operate locally in your browser sandbox, eliminating server queues and latency.

Visit [Versa PDF](https://versapdf.com) to experience truly private, zero-upload document workflows.
